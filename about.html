Who We Are

AETCT Exchange (Aetheris Cryptotech Global) is a global digital asset trading platform dedicated to creating a secure, transparent, and compliance-driven financial ecosystem.

Headquartered in Houston, Texas, AETCT Exchange focuses on providing institutional-grade infrastructure, advanced trading technology, and user-centered solutions for investors worldwide.

Our platform is designed to bridge innovation and trust by combining modern financial technology with strong operational standards and responsible governance.
